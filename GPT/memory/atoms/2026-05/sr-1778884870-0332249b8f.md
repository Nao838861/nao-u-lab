# Bounded Autonomy: Controlling LLM Characters in Live Multiplayer Games

## Use when

Use when designing LLM NPC or AI companion behavior that must remain grounded in world state and player intent. Bounded Autonomy separates agent-agent interaction, agent-world action execution, and player-agent steering, with decay, grounding, fallback, and whisper style soft steering.

## Excerpt

The candidate treats LLM characters as a control-interface problem, not just free-form conversation. It separates social interaction, world-action grounding, and player steering, then evaluates stability, grounding quality, and intervention success in live multiplayer play. For Nao_u_BOT, this becomes a small probe for NPC action sets, fallback behavior, and lightweight steering signals.
