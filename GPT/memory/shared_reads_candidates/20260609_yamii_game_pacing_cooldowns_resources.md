## raw_excerpt
Search result excerpt from yamii. The article treats cooldowns and resources as tools for shaping the rhythm of play. Cooldowns add timed pauses after abilities so players cannot spam strong actions; resources such as mana, stamina, ammo, or energy force choices over a longer arc. The excerpt emphasizes action/rest waves: high-energy moments peak, cooldowns or depleted resources pull the player back toward planning, then the next opportunity creates renewed tension.

The article separates per-ability cooldowns, global cooldowns, dynamic cooldowns, regeneration, pickups, shared pools, and layered resources. It also notes that invisible waits feel unfair, so progress bars, color changes, sound cues, and haptics can make cooldowns readable. For candidate purposes, this is a practical pacing checklist for tuning prototypes where the raw mechanic works but the moment-to-moment rhythm is either spammy, flat, or frustrating.
