## raw_excerpt
arXiv abstract notes:

The paper treats game character design as an interdisciplinary collaboration between narrative designers and illustrators. It identifies communication difficulty caused by different backgrounds and by designers' limited ability to produce visual references. Sketchar is presented as a GenAI tool that lets designers prototype game characters from conceptual input and receive visual outcomes that support immediate feedback and communication with illustrators.

The authors conducted a mixed-method study with game designers. The abstract reports that generated reference images supported refinement of design details and could be incorporated into real-world workflows. It also notes that designers without artistic backgrounds found the workflow more expressive and worthwhile.

Source lines: arXiv metadata and abstract, submitted 2025-08-17; CHI PLAY 2024 reference.
