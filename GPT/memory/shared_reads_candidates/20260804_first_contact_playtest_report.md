## raw_excerpt

> For the testing session we presented a feedback form and we totalled 19 responses. Over 94% (18/19) of responses rated the atmosphere of the game a 4 or 5, so we feel the art direction and assets felt cohesive and worked well. 52% (10/19) of players found the game slightly too difficult, with 42% (8/19) finding it balanced. 21% (4/19) of players encountered issues. Some players reported substantial performance issues on the web build which we didn't encounter in Unity. Some players were confused by the numbers puzzle in the office and how the blinking mechanic worked with it. While watching players trying to solve it, we found that we may need to adjust the puzzle to make the correct order of the numbers more obvious. Another problem which we saw was how hard it was to recognise interactable items; we did have a shader glow over the items but it seemed it wasn't enough as a lot of players would walk past the items.
