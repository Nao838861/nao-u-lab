## raw_excerpt

> This update is called the v0.3 Hybrid Production Update, and it is probably one of the most important design passes so far. The big goal was to make the game feel more like a tiny tabletop RTS, where your buildings are not just passive resource cards, but actual pieces of your village. In earlier versions, Production Buildings mostly existed to make resources, while other buildings handled infrastructure tags like Muster, Depot, Workshop, Den, and so on. In v0.3, some Production Buildings now do both. Ruining a hybrid Production Building does not just slow the opponent's economy. It can also shut off the infrastructure tags they need to play key cards. The second major rule update is First Yield. When you construct a Production Building, you immediately gain its printed unconditional Harvest resource once. The goal here is to make early turns smoother without making the economy spiral out of control.
