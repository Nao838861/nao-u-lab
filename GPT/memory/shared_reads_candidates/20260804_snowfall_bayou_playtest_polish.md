## raw_excerpt

> Playtest, Polish, Playtest, Polish. This sprint has been a sprint to be pushing in and polishing out our systems to support our core loop and get to the finish line. To start with changes made based on feedback, we added a tutorial system to the game to now guide the player through the process of learning the games systems and mechanics. furthermore, to help the player know what items and materials go where there was a design change for items to show what modules they can be put into if you hover your mouse over them. Next up Boring Farming. Farming is not interesting, to improve this we are adding in micro minigames to farming and increasing visual effect amount on module process to feel more engaging and interactive to do. Players don't realize they can eat food during playtesting so adding eating mechanics to quests will be important to show players what foods can be eaten. Players also need to be taught TAB opens their inventory.
